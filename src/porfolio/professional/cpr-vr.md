A simulation of a CPR scenario for collaborative scientific research, it checks the effectiveness of VR when it comes to teaching real life skills compared to traditional solutions (e.g. a [Resusci Anne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resusci_Anne)). Volunteers are students from the Arab American University and the research took 4 weeks. VR Headsets: Meta Quest 2 and Pro. Made with Unity.

Contains a solution to localize Right to Left languages correctly on UI elements from Right to Left.
