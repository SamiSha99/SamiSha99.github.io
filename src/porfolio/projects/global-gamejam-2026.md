My first Game Jam, I worked with 3 other participants where we focused on making a game in less than 48 hours.

We created a platformer game called Maskable, where the player needs to switch the world with a different one to overcome the level. It contains 5 levels.

Chaos is modifier is a local modifier during my time in Tiltan where I am learning for Game Design, the modifier allows us to focus on an additional theme, unfortunately our game didn't meet the criteria, but considering we were 1st year students, we were considered very new and had no idea how to manage making a game in 48 hours.

If anything, I'm even glad we got a working game prototype that is playable and lots of people found the idea fascinating and interesting with lots of potential.

Perhaps one day, I'll explore this idea again and make a separate game out of it.
